You Are Invited to Our Wedding

We’re happy to invite you to celebrate our wedding with us in the heart of the Carpathian Mountains. From May 14 to 16, 2027, we’ll spend a weekend together surrounded by nature, fresh mountain air, good food, music, and great company. We would love for you to be part of these special days and create unforgettable memories together. Looking forward to celebrating with you.

The location

A beautiful mountain region in western Ukraine

The Carpathians are a peaceful mountain region in western Ukraine, with forested hills, open mountain views, and a rich local culture shaped by music, craft, and mountain traditions. It is also the region where Antonina was born, which makes it a special place for us to bring everyone together. We are currently looking for the right venue to host us for the weekend: a place that feels beautiful, safe, and comfortable for our guests, while also being practical from a travel and logistics point of view.

Exact venue details will follow once guest numbers are confirmed.

Wedding attire

A touch of Ukrainian tradition

Our wedding will have a touch of traditional Ukrainian spirit: a church ceremony, live music featuring classic Ukrainian wedding songs, joyful festivities, delicious local food, dancing, and, most importantly, a warm and vibrant atmosphere shared with all of you.

Vyshyvanka

We will not have a dress code. However, many of our Ukrainian guests will likely wear traditional embroidered clothing ("vyshyvanka"), and we would be delighted if some of our international guests would also like to join this beautiful tradition. If you are interested, we would be happy to recommend where you can order or pre-order embroidered dresses, shirts, or jewellery. It could also be a wonderful keepsake from your trip to Ukraine and our celebration together. Please reach out to us directly if you would like recommendations.
