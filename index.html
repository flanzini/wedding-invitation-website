The location

A beautiful mountain region in western Ukraine

The Carpathians are a peaceful mountain region in western Ukraine, with forested hills, open mountain views, and a rich local culture shaped by music, craft, and mountain traditions. It is also the region where Antonina was born, which makes it a special place for us to bring everyone together. As we plan the weekend, we are choosing the venue with care. Our main goal is to find a place that is beautiful, safe, comfortable for guests, and practical from a travel and logistics point of view.

Wedding attire

A touch of Ukrainian tradition, if you wish

Our wedding will have a touch of traditional Ukrainian spirit: a church ceremony, live music featuring classic Ukrainian wedding songs, joyful festivities, delicious local food, dancing, and, most importantly, a warm and vibrant atmosphere shared with all of you. We will share more details about the wedding day programme closer to the date, but we hope this already gives you a little glimpse into the vibe of the celebration.

Vyshyvanka

We will not have a strict wedding attire requirement. However, many of our Ukrainian guests will likely wear traditional embroidered clothing ("vyshyvanka"), and we would be delighted if some of our international guests would also like to join this beautiful tradition. If you are interested, we would be happy to recommend lovely and affordable stores where you can order or pre-order embroidered dresses, shirts, or jewellery. It could also be a wonderful keepsake from your trip to Ukraine and our celebration together, while many modern versions of vyshyvanka are stylish enough to become part of your everyday wardrobe long after the wedding. Please reach out to us directly if you would like recommendations.

Wedding guest attire

Of course, if traditional Ukrainian clothing is not your style, that is absolutely fine; simply come dressed as you would for any wedding celebration. As the wedding ceremony will take place in a church, we kindly ask guests to choose respectful attire and avoid very short dresses, shorts, or exposed shoulders during the ceremony.
